## 2. High-priority improvements

Four grouped issues should lead the next authorized editorial phase:

1. **OP-06 — Reconcile Contact's booking contradiction.** The page promises follow-up from Book Now while its final CTA says submission is not connected. Have the operational owner verify the actual production flow, then approve one accurate explanation. Acceptance: hero, form instructions and final CTA agree; do not claim successful request delivery without an authorized test.
2. **OP-07 — Replace service-page planning placeholders.** `/services/appliance-repair` and `/services/water-heater-repair` should explain actual confirmed service scope. Use the supplied appliance inventory already in the footer; verify water-heater equipment support before naming it. Acceptance: no future-development language, useful scope and exclusions, clear next step, no new unsupported service pages.
3. **OP-08 — Deepen the eight detail pages.** Obtain approved inspection scope, customer preparation, included/excluded work, decision factors, examples and specific FAQs. AC maintenance should explain its relationship to the advertised outdoor-unit cleaning. Furnace and boiler should explain distinct equipment work. Acceptance: each page answers its own service question with substantive unique information; shared process/region blocks remain concise. Do not target an arbitrary word count.
4. **OP-10 — Resolve Apex's provider role.** About describes independent providers but says “We diagnose”; Service schemas name Apex as provider. The owner should establish accurate coordination/provider terminology before an editor or developer changes it. Acceptance: visible copy and structured relationships agree without inventing credentials, offices or service responsibilities.

Future opportunities require evidence and roadmap authorization: approved local project examples, more informative reviews, verified company identity and service-process detail, and measurement of organic queries/conversions. Expand an existing page before proposing another route. Do not build thin county, appliance or brand pages, invent service areas, or add integrations as a side effect of this audit.

LocalBusiness is conditional: the site has no verified address and describes coordination with independent providers. Google's feature requires name/address; do not fabricate either or select a contractor subtype based only on keywords ([LocalBusiness documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business)). FAQPage is not a missing rich-result opportunity: Google's current changelog states FAQ rich results ended in May 2026 ([documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)). Keep useful visible FAQs without promising that search treatment.
