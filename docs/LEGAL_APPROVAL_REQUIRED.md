# Owner information and approval required

The old Privacy Policy and Terms URLs returned 404 because no corresponding App Router pages existed. New `/privacy-policy` and `/terms` are explicit draft structures, noindex and excluded from the sitemap. They are not operative policies and do not make the site legally ready. No effective date, jurisdiction, contractual guarantees or retention promises have been invented.

Obtain written owner-approved copy and facts for:

- Legal operator name, DBA relationship, business address where applicable, support contact and monitored privacy contact.
- Apex's exact role versus independent service providers; recipient identities/categories, responsibility and sharing arrangements.
- Confirmed fields/purposes, cookies and analytics/advertising vendors, consent choices, user-provided-data use, rights handling, deletion and retention schedules.
- Call/SMS practices, recording and consent wording if applicable; whether Quo records any calls; marketing versus operational communications.
- Diagnostic fees and offer conditions, estimates, cancellations/refunds, payment recipient, warranties, liability and dispute terms approved by the owner's legal reviewer.
- Applicable service jurisdictions, licenses/insurance/credentials and permission for public claims/reviews. Verify public phone, hours and availability promises.

Approval must identify the approver, exact content/version and approval date. Then replace the draft content, remove pending labels and reconsider indexing. Do not simply remove the warning or noindex from these empty structures. Independent legal/compliance review and production deployment are outside this remediation.
