# Apex Solution

Apex Solution is a premium, scalable US home-services website focused on HVAC, plumbing, indoor air quality, and high-conversion service booking.

## Product promise

The experience combines technical precision, trustworthy local service, editorial polish, and problem-first navigation. The brand line is **Service at a higher standard.**

## Current delivery boundary

Phase 0 established the production foundation. Phase 1 establishes the complete responsive homepage, accessible global navigation, local temporary media system, and primary visual language. Internal service, booking, company, location, resource, and legal routes remain `noindex` foundation pages until their implementation phases.

## Confirmed versus pending content

Confirmed from the brief: brand name, positioning, initial palette, service taxonomy, Phoenix-area route list, and the Apex Care concept.

Pending business verification: phone, email, street address, hours, licenses, emergency availability, prices, financing terms, guarantees, statistics, reviews, projects, team members, offers, and legal copy. These must not be invented.

## Local commands
